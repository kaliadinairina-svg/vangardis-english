Privacy Policy

Version of 16 November 2026

1. Who processes your data

Vangardis, attorneys at law (hereinafter — the Firm), acting as the controller of personal data. For anything concerning data processing, write to office@vangardis.ua or call +38 (067) 408-25-60.

2. What data we collect

Through the call-back form on this website:

  • the name or form of address you provide;
  • a phone number for the call back.

The form collects nothing else. The website deliberately does not collect details of your case — those are better discussed by phone or in person.

3. On what grounds and for what purpose

The legal basis is your consent, given by ticking the box before you submit the form. The box is not pre-ticked: consent is given by a deliberate action.

The purpose is to call you back and establish whether you need legal assistance. The data is used for nothing else: we send no newsletters and pass no contacts to third parties for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

If you do not become a client after the conversation, the contact is deleted within 30 days. If an agreement on legal assistance is concluded, the data is kept for the periods set by the legislation on advocacy and on archival storage.

5. Who we share it with

Personal data is not shared with third parties, except where the law expressly requires it, and except with the attorney of the Firm who will handle your case.

A separate reminder: information entrusted to an attorney is privileged and protected by Article 22 of the Law of Ukraine “On the Bar and Practice of Law”.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website uses no cookies, runs no visitor counters and loads no web analytics or advertising pixels. Your activity on the site is not tracked.

7. Your rights

Under the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection” you have the right to:

  • know exactly which of your data we process;
  • obtain a copy of that data;
  • demand correction of inaccurate data;
  • withdraw consent and demand deletion of the data;
  • lodge a complaint about data processing with the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights.

To exercise any of these rights, write to office@vangardis.ua. We reply within 30 calendar days.

8. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the version date at the top of the page is updated. Material changes affecting the purpose of processing are agreed with the people whose data is already stored.

Information on this website is not legal advice.
