Frequently asked questions

How much does an attorney cost?

Attending a search or an arrest starts at UAH 8,000; a consultation starts at UAH 2,000. The fee for running a case is quoted after the first consultation and fixed in the engagement letter.

Can I hire an attorney for a relative?

Yes. A family member can sign the engagement, and the client confirms it afterwards.

I already have a state-appointed attorney. Can I replace them?

You have the right to bring in a defense attorney of your own choosing at any moment.

Do you work outside Kyiv?

We travel across the Kyiv region. Elsewhere — online, and in person by arrangement.

Who will know that I contacted you?

No one. The very fact that you contacted an attorney is privileged.
